# EEO Comms and Culture Principles

> Source: Engineering, Enablement, and Operations (EEO) Comms Guidance

---

## Guiding Principles

1. **One brand, one culture: Microsoft.** All communication reflects a unified identity.
2. **Trust and empower leaders** to shape and manage their business.
3. **Use employee listening systems** to guide priorities and activations.
4. **Leverage what exists** before building something new.
5. **Integrate comms and culture efforts** into existing rhythm of business and channels whenever possible.
6. **Empower leaders** to shape and manage their team culture based on unique needs.
7. **Relevance threshold:** EEO-wide culture initiatives and comms should be relevant for >75% of the organization or targeted community.

---

## Where to Find Updates

- The majority of EEO-wide comms are posted on **Viva Engage**.
- Major announcements come from **Kim Akers by email**, sometimes followed by team-specific details from your leader.
- Continue to follow communications from **your leaders and manager**.

## Comms Platform Principles

We aim to spread the right message to the right people at the right time and in the right way.

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Relevant | Content applies to ~75% of the organization or the targeted community |
| Priority-aligned | Messages reflect our priorities and ambitions |
| Inclusive and accessible | Compliant by default |
| Transparent | Accurate and timely information that builds confidence and trust |
| Clear and simple | Easy to understand |

## Platform Usage Guide

| Platform | Logo/ID | Primary Use | When to Use |
|----------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| **Viva Engage** | Viva Engage | EEO-wide and people manager communities | Share updates, have conversations, community building |
| **SharePoint Hub** | SharePoint | Central hub of evergreen content, resources, links | Leader messages, event recaps, calendar, time-specific info |
| **Teams** | Teams | Small group collaboration | Behind-the-scenes work, project coordination |
| **Outlook** | Outlook | Formal communications | Announcements, policy changes, staffing updates, Viva Engage auto-notifications |
| **In-Office Spaces** | Physical | Culture and core messaging | Experimenting with building spaces to bring culture to life |
