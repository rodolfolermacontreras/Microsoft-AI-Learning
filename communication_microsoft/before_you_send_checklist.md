# Before You Hit Send -- Comms Checklist

> Use this checklist before sending any email, publishing any post, or delivering any presentation. Adapted from EEO Comms Guidance.

---

## 1. Audience

- [ ] Have you considered your audience?
- [ ] Have you been mindful of the audience's knowledge, interests, and needs?
- [ ] Have you written in a tone and level of detail that will resonate with them?

---

## 2. Purpose and Clarity

- [ ] Is the purpose clear?
- [ ] Have you provided relevant context?

---

## 3. Organization and Structure

- [ ] Is the content clearly organized?
- [ ] Did you start with the most important points?
- [ ] Did you use headings, bullet points, or numbered lists for readability?
- [ ] Does the content maintain logical flow from one idea to the next?

---

## 4. Conciseness

- [ ] Did you use short sentences?
- [ ] Did you avoid jargon and overly technical terms?
- [ ] Are you using data that could distract from the main message? (If so, simplify or move to appendix.)
- [ ] Have you used active voice and actionable language?

---

## 5. Call to Action

- [ ] Did you specify any actions required?
- [ ] Have you been clear on deadlines and next steps?

---

## 6. Accuracy and Completeness

- [ ] Have you double-checked for accuracy and completeness?
- [ ] Are all hyperlinks working and accessible for your audience?
- [ ] Did you use AKA links where appropriate?
- [ ] Do you have the right email aliases, and do you have permissions to send?

---

## 7. Tone

- [ ] Does your message match the intended tone?
- [ ] Have you avoided overly informal or casual language?
- [ ] Did you read the message out loud to hear how it reads?

---

## 8. Proofreading

- [ ] Did you check for grammar, punctuation, and spelling errors?
- [ ] Did you have someone proofread your message?
- [ ] Are you using the same typeface/font throughout?

---

## 9. Inclusivity and Accessibility

- [ ] Have you run Accessibility Checker and resolved all issues?
- [ ] Are you using inclusive language?
- [ ] Have you been inclusive in any imagery you feature?

---

## Quick Self-Test

Before hitting send, answer these three questions:

1. **What is the ONE thing** I want the reader to take away?
2. **What should they DO** after reading this?
3. **Would I understand this** if I had no prior context?

If any answer is unclear, revise.
